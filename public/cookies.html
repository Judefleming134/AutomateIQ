Cookies Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They can be essential (needed for the site to work) or non-essential (analytics, advertising and similar).

2. Cookies we use

Marketing website (automateiq.ie): no cookies are set. We run no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on the public website.

Platform (portal & admin): strictly necessary authentication cookies only, set by our authentication provider (Supabase) when you log in. They keep you signed in securely and are removed when you sign out or they expire. Because they are strictly necessary for a service you request, they do not require consent under the ePrivacy rules.

3. Third-party content

Fonts on the public website are loaded from Google Fonts, which involves a request to Google's servers but does not set cookies. If we ever add non-essential cookies (for example analytics), we will update this policy and ask for your consent first with a cookie banner.

4. Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking the platform's authentication cookies will prevent you from logging in.
